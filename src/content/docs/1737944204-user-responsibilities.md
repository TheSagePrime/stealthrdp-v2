1. **Account security:** Users are responsible for maintaining the security and confidentiality of their account credentials. Any activities under the account are the user's responsibility. Promptly inform StealthRDP of any unauthorized use of your account.
2. **Compliance with laws:** Users must comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and these Terms and Conditions when using StealthRDP services. This includes, but is not limited to, adhering to copyright, data protection, and anti-abuse laws.
3. **Acceptable use:** Adhere to the acceptable use policy as outlined in the "Use of Service" section. Any misuse or unethical behavior may result in suspension or termination of services.
4. **Data management:** Users are responsible for backing up their data. StealthRDP is not liable for any data loss or corruption.
5. **Service usage:** Use the services provided for lawful, intended purposes. Any illegal or unauthorized use of the services is strictly prohibited.
6. **Communication:** Maintain respectful and professional communication with StealthRDP staff and other users. Abusive or disrespectful behavior will not be tolerated.
7. **Reporting issues:** Report any service-related issues or vulnerabilities to StealthRDP promptly to allow for timely resolutions.
8. **Resource usage:** Use the provided resources (like bandwidth and storage) responsibly, ensuring fair usage as per the terms outlined in the "Bandwidth Usage Policy".
9. **Updates and notifications:** Keep your contact information up to date and respond to notifications or inquiries from StealthRDP in a timely manner.
10. **Weekly backups:** We have weekly backups for disaster recovery. However, please DO NOT use your server as a storage server for really important data. There is always a chance of servers being unresponsive or having hardware issues. Do regular backups; this is really important. We are not able to restore files, for example if the server has any hardware issues. It is your job to keep your data safe.

11. **Microsoft licensing:** StealthRDP provides the infrastructure only. Microsoft Windows licensing is not included and is not supplied by StealthRDP. Customers using Windows are responsible for obtaining and maintaining any Microsoft licences required for their intended use. Customers may use their own eligible Microsoft licences where permitted by Microsoft's applicable licensing terms. Customers are responsible for determining whether their licence is valid for their intended hosted deployment. See [Windows licensing](/docs/windows-licensing).

By creating an account and using StealthRDP's services, you accept these responsibilities and agree to adhere to them as part of our service agreement.
