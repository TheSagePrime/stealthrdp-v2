Payment Terms

Payment Terms
=============

Last updated on Mar 13, 2025

1.  Accepted Payment Method: StealthRDP accepts only cryptocurrency and Card Payment as a form of payment for all services.
    
2.  Cryptocurrency Transactions: Customers are responsible for transaction fees and sending the correct amount. Transactions must be completed within the invoice timeframe.
    
3.  Future Payment Methods: Should additional payment methods be introduced, they will adhere to these terms unless otherwise specified.
    
4.  Payment Obligations: Full upfront payment is required. Delayed payment may lead to service suspension or termination.
    
5.  Refunds: A new service can be refunded within 7 days of payment. Refunds are issued only as account credit to the customer's site wallet with StealthRDP; they are not paid back to the original payment method, and direct cryptocurrency refunds are not provided under any circumstances. After 7 days, payments to StealthRDP are non-refundable. In exceptional cases of internal issues leading to service downtime, compensations or refunds may be issued equitably as account credit, but these do not cover personal damages. Services are to be provided within 24 hours of payment; should service delivery exceed this timeframe, customers are eligible to request a refund. Refunds are granted only once per service.
    
6.  Transaction Confirmations: Service activation follows the minimum required confirmations for the respective cryptocurrency, which can vary in time. StealthRDP is not responsible for blockchain delays.
    
7.  Crypto Gateway Payment Terms: All payments processed through our site are handled via our designated crypto gateway, coinPayment. When making a payment, please note that all associated fees are the responsibility of the payer. You must send the exact amount—or a slightly higher amount—as displayed on the payment page; sending any lower amount than specified will be considered an incomplete payment, requiring the client to complete the payment again or request a refund through the gateway. The coinPayment gateway reserves the right to deduct any applicable fees, and StealthRDP is not responsible for refunds or fee adjustments made by the gateway. If you experience any issues with your payment, please contact the coinPayment support team directly using the email provided in your transaction confirmation.
    
8.  Changes to Payment Terms: StealthRDP may revise these terms with immediate effect, communicated in advance to customers.
    

By making a payment, you accept these terms.
