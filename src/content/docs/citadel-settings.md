## Organization and team

Review plan entitlements in Settings. Invite teammates with the role they need. Verify your email if prompted, because security mail requires a verified address.

## Notifications

Choose email events such as attack start or end and awaiting DNS. Add a webhook URL for attack or lockdown events, then send a test delivery to verify it reaches your endpoint.

For invoices, renewals or a human ticket, use [billing and support](/citadel/docs/billing-support).
