## General use

Customers are permitted to use StealthRDP's services, including Remote Desktop Protocols (RDPs), Virtual Private Servers (VPS), and web hosting solutions, for lawful purposes as intended under these Terms and Conditions. The services are to be used in a manner that is consistent with the intended purpose and should not interfere with the use and enjoyment of others.

## Permitted activities

You may use our services to conduct business, personal, and organizational activities provided they are legal, ethical, and in accordance with these terms.

## Prohibited activities

As part of our commitment to providing a high-quality and lawful service environment, StealthRDP enforces a strict policy against the following activities:

- Engaging in behaviors that could lead to a Spamhaus listing.
- Establishing or operating phishing or scam sites, including but not limited to impersonation of entities such as eBay, PayPal, or any schemes involving credit card or password fraud.
- Carding, fraud, or any other financial crime activities.
- Conducting network scanning or port scanning activities.
- Hosting, distributing, or running malicious scripts intended for hacking, initiating DDoS attacks, or any other unlawful intrusion activities.
- Participating in or facilitating DDoS/DoS attacks.
- Engaging in the dissemination, hosting, or promotion of child pornography or similar exploitative content.
- Operating botnets, Remote Administration Tools (RATs), or any related malicious activities.

### Consequences of violation

:::warn
Any breach of these terms will lead to immediate service suspension without backups. Depending on the severity of the breach, StealthRDP reserves the right to close your customer account and terminate all associated active services.
:::

## Abuse notifications

StealthRDP retains the right to suspend or terminate your services without prior notice upon receipt of abuse complaints. Our decision will be based on the nature of the complaint and the evidence provided.

## Bandwidth usage policy

- **Allocated bandwidth:** Usage is confined to the limits specified on the product page.
- **Fair share policy:** We operate a fair share policy to ensure service quality for all users. Excessive use of bandwidth that affects others is not permitted.
- **Usage monitoring:** We monitor usage to prevent network congestion and maintain service quality.
- **Consequences of excessive use:** Excessive usage may result in bandwidth throttling, service suspension, or mandatory upgrades at additional costs.
- **Remedial actions:** Repeated overuse after warnings may lead to service termination.
- **Bandwidth reports:** Customers can request bandwidth usage reports from our support team.
- Compliance with this policy is mandatory for continued use of StealthRDP services.

By using StealthRDP's services, you agree to adhere to the above usage policy. Any misuse of the service will be addressed promptly and may result in immediate termination of services and legal action where necessary.

## Windows licensing

StealthRDP provides the infrastructure only. Microsoft Windows licensing is not included and is not supplied by StealthRDP, including SPLA licences, RDS licences, activation keys, or licensing services, even if requested. Where Windows Server Evaluation is used, it is Evaluation software, not a permanently licensed Windows installation. Customers using Windows are responsible for obtaining and maintaining any Microsoft licences required for their intended use. Customers may use their own eligible Microsoft licences where permitted by Microsoft's applicable licensing terms. Customers are responsible for determining whether their licence is valid for their intended hosted deployment. See [Windows licensing](/docs/windows-licensing).
