## Accuracy controls

- Product-specific facts are limited to current repo/public-site truth sources.
- No invented benchmarks, search volume, rankings, customer proof, or performance guarantees.
- Trading article is infrastructure-only and explicitly avoids investment outcome claims.
- Remote desktop article does not claim Windows licensing is included.
- Backup article separates backup-target flexibility from durability/recovery guarantees.
- Automation article separates VM availability from job success and states abuse boundaries.

## Cannibalization controls

- Remote desktop: existing security/performance posts keep their narrower intents.
- Backups: existing automated-backup post keeps implementation/setup intent.
- Web hosting, automation and trading do not have existing pages with the same core search task.
