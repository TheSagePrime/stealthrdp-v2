# SEO decisions

Judgment calls, with the reason, so a later session does not re-argue them. Newest first.

## 2026-10-05 — Owner decisions for German and Spanish

- **No Windows licence add-on.** Pages say plainly that the licence is not included, even though German
  competitors sell "Lizenz inklusive".
- **GDPR compliance approved for Amsterdam and Phoenix** ("DSGVO-konform", "conforme al RGPD").
- **"günstig", "barato" and "económico" are allowed** in German and Spanish. The English ban on "cheap"
  stays.
- **Language add-ons:** install 7 of the 20 SEOO skills, each with a note that `AGENTS.md` and
  `PRODUCT_FACTS.md` win. The other 13 overlap with repo checks or do not fit an early-stage site yet.

## 2026-10-05 — German and Spanish, as subfolders, research-led

- Languages: German and Spanish first. Owner wants French, Italian and Dutch later, and Russian only after
  a payment and legal check.
- URLs: subfolders `/de` and `/es` on www.stealthrdp.com with hreflang, not separate domains, so they
  inherit the domain's authority. English URLs stay unchanged.
- No redirect by visitor country (Google crawls mostly from the US); offer a language switch instead.
- Pages are written from local keyword research, not translated line by line. Core pages first, then
  guides and docs in batches every 2-3 days, so publishing never looks like a burst.
- Spanish targets Spain (es-ES). Mexico was too small and "rdp" there means a medicine.
- AI writes the pages; no native reviewer for now (owner).
