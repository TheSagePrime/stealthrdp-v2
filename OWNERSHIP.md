# Ownership

Sage Prime `web-starter` repository and is now a separate product repository.

Sage Prime owns the original code, product identity, configuration and frontend in this repository.

Third-party packages remain third-party. Their licenses do not transfer to Sage Prime.
See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for the dependency and adapted-source boundary.

Do not add provider credentials, customer data or product secrets to Git.
